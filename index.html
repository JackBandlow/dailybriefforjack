What this is

The Daily Brief is a short, neutral, fact-based news email that arrives early every weekday morning. Monday's issue covers the weekend.

Each section you pick has 2 to 4 major stories of 60 to 90 words, then up to 5 quick updates of one or two sentences. There is no filler and nothing padded.

Every item is independently researched and double-checked, and every item links to its sources.
