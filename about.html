Daily Brief for Jack

What it is

Daily Brief for Jack is a free weekday news email and website. Each issue is a neutral, fact-based summary of the day’s news in the topics you choose, with every item linked to its published sources.

Who runs it

Daily Brief for Jack is a free weekday news email by Jack Bandlow. It was created to automate its owner’s daily news search.

How it is produced

Each issue is generated using automated processes designed to be truthful, neutral, and based on at least two independent published sources, which are linked. The content summarizes reporting by third parties. Automated tools can make mistakes; please check the linked sources before relying on anything here.
