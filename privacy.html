Privacy

Effective November 3, 2026

This page describes what information Daily Brief for Jack (dailybriefforjack.com) handles and how.

What is collected

  • Your email address, when you sign up, so the daily email can be sent to you.
  • Your chosen topics, so your email includes the sections you picked.
  • Standard server logs. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as IP addresses when pages are visited. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Email service

Subscriptions are processed and emails are sent by Kit (kit.com), an email service provider. Your email address and topic choices are stored with Kit. Kit may record information about email delivery and activity under its own policies; see Kit’s privacy policy.

How to unsubscribe

Use the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. To change topics, use the topics page. You can also email the contact address below and ask to be removed.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: jack@dailybriefforjack.com.

Changes

If this page changes, the effective date above will be updated.
