Level Up

Swap rereading for a 5-minute self-test

After reading a case, spec or training plan, close it and write from memory the three to five points you'd need to explain it, then check what you missed and reread only those parts. In a 2013 review of 10 study techniques, Dunlosky and colleagues rated practice testing and spaced practice "high utility" and rereading and highlighting "low utility." Lab work by Roediger and Karpicke (2006) found that taking memory tests improved long-term retention more than restudying the same material. Repeat the quiz a day later and again a week later, for example on a new Intapp module or a federal procedure rule, so the spacing works alongside the testing.

Psychological Science in the Public Interest · Psychological Science

Batch messages into set check-in times to cut switching costs

Pick two or three fixed times a day to process email and chat (say 9:00, 12:30 and 4:00), and close or mute those apps during deep-work blocks. Task-switching experiments summarized by the American Psychological Association show people lose time every time they switch, with bigger losses on complex or unfamiliar tasks. In a UC Irvine study (Mark, Gudith and Klocke, 2008), interrupted people finished tasks just as fast as uninterrupted ones by working faster, but reported significantly more stress, frustration, time pressure and effort. Before each block, write one line on what "done" looks like so you know where to pick up when you return.

American Psychological Association · UC Irvine CHI 2008

Today's field: Law (U.S. legal concepts)

  • Res judicata (claim preclusion). Once a court has issued a final judgment on the merits, the same parties can't sue each other again over the same claim. It's like a referee's final call: you can appeal it, but you can't replay the game in front of a new referee. If a plaintiff loses, the claim is barred; if the plaintiff wins, the claim is "merged" into the judgment, so they can't sue again for more damages. Some dismissals, like those for lack of jurisdiction or ones expressly "without prejudice," don't trigger it. It is separate from issue preclusion, which blocks relitigating a particular issue that was already decided. Cornell LII Wex · Wikipedia
  • Promissory estoppel. Usually a promise needs something in exchange, called consideration, before it becomes an enforceable contract. Promissory estoppel is an exception: if someone reasonably relies on a promise to their detriment, the promisor could have foreseen that reliance, and enforcing the promise is needed to avoid injustice, a court can make the promisor pay. For example, if a promise leads someone to sell property or spend significant money and the promise is then broken, that person may recover even without a formal contract. The American Law Institute wrote the principle into Section 90 of the Restatement of Contracts in 1932. Cornell LII Wex · Wikipedia
