Energy & Climate

Climate summit closes without an overarching agreement

The UN climate conference in Antalya ended Sunday without agreement on a common closing text. Delegates remained divided over how to address fossil fuels and how to finance action in vulnerable countries. The breakdown concerned the overall political agreement. Negotiators still reached narrower decisions, including work on adaptation. That distinction matters for governments preparing for floods or drought: technical work can continue even when countries cannot agree on a shared commitment about energy use.

Today's field: AI & machine learning

  • Training and inference. Training changes a model’s parameters using examples. Inference uses the resulting parameters to produce an output, such as classifying a new image. [Google Machine Learning Glossary]
  • Precision. Precision asks what fraction of the examples a model marked positive actually belonged in that group. For a spam filter, it describes how often a message labeled spam really was spam. [Google Machine Learning Glossary]
  • Recall. Recall asks what fraction of all actual positives the model found. A filter that flags just one obvious spam message can be precise while letting most spam through. [Google Machine Learning Glossary]
