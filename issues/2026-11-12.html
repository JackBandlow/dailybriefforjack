Democrat Scott Colom unseats Hyde-Smith in Mississippi Senate race

Democrat Scott Colom unseated Republican Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith, becoming Mississippi's first Black senator since Reconstruction and the first Democrat elected to the Senate there since 1982. The Associated Press called the race Wednesday, more than a week after Election Day, with Colom at 49.2% to Hyde-Smith's 48.7%, a margin of just over 4,600 votes of more than 960,000 cast. Hyde-Smith conceded Wednesday afternoon. The win is the eighth Senate seat Democrats have flipped and gives them a majority of at least 55.

Level Up

How to start with the vital few on a long list

The Pareto principle, or 80:20 rule, states that for many outcomes, roughly 80% of consequences come from 20% of causes. Joseph M. Juran developed the idea in quality control in 1941 after reading Vilfredo Pareto. The split is only a shorthand; in individual cases it can be nearer to 90:10 or 70:30. When a list is long, start with the few items that would drive most of the result; Juran later preferred "the vital few and the useful many."

Sources: wikipedia.org · arxiv.org

How to watch both ends of the Treasury yield curve

A yield curve shows the relationship between yields and time to maturity for comparable debt securities, such as the US Treasury curve. The shape can change when short-term and long-term yields do not move in step. The two-year Treasury yield rose 4 basis points to 4.765%, while the 10-year yield was up 0.3 basis points at 4.991%, according to Tradeweb. When you check rates, look at both ends of the curve, not a single yield, to see whether the gap is widening or narrowing.

Today's field: Finance & economics

  • Federal funds rate. The federal funds rate is the interest rate at which banks and credit unions lend reserve balances to each other overnight on an uncollateralized basis. Reserve balances are amounts held at the Federal Reserve. Institutions with surplus balances lend to institutions that need larger balances, and the rate is a benchmark that influences a wide range of market interest rates in the United States. The Federal Open Market Committee sets a target range for the rate at meetings that normally occur eight times a year. [wikipedia.org · federalreserve.gov]
  • Market capitalization. Market capitalization, or market cap, is the total value of a publicly traded company's outstanding common shares. It equals the market price per common share multiplied by the number of common shares outstanding. It is sometimes used to rank the size of companies, but it measures only the equity component of a company's capital structure and does not reflect how much debt the firm uses. [wikipedia.org · investopedia.com]
  • Yield curve. A yield curve shows the relationship between yields and time to maturity for a set of comparable debt securities. In practice the term usually refers to curves from a single issuer or market segment, such as the US Treasury curve for government bonds. Analysts often summarize the curve's shape with a term spread, such as the difference between the 10-year Treasury yield and the 3-month Treasury bill rate. A sustained inversion, when short-term interest rates are higher than long-term rates, has historically preceded US recessions. [wikipedia.org · duke.edu]
