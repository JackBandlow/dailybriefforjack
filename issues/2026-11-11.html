How to ground a model answer in documents you retrieve first

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is a technique that lets large language models retrieve information from external data sources and then respond to a query. These documents supplement the model's training data, so it can use domain-specific or updated information that is not in that training data. Ars Technica describes RAG as blending the model process with a document look-up to help the model stick to the facts, and says it is not a direct solution because the model can still hallucinate around the source material. Before you ask a model about your own files, retrieve the relevant passages and place them in the prompt with the question.

How to stop a task from filling all the time you leave open

Parkinson's Law is the observation, from a 1955 essay by naval historian C. Northcote Parkinson in The Economist, that work expands to fill the available time. The same essay said an official wants to multiply subordinates, not rivals, and that officials make work for each other. A practical step is to give important work a fixed end time shorter than the open calendar slot, then stop or re-plan when that time is up.

Today's field: Construction & engineering

  • Geotextile. Geotextiles are permeable fabrics that, used with soil, can separate layers, filter water, reinforce ground, protect a surface, or provide drainage. They are typically crafted from polypropylene or polyester, in two primary forms: woven, which resembles mail bag sacking, and nonwoven, which resembles felt. They were originally intended as a substitute for granular soil filters. They are used in roads, railroads, embankments, dams, and construction site silt fences. Products such as geogrids are grouped with them as geosynthetics. [wikipedia.org · nih.gov]
  • Cantilever. A cantilever is a structural element that is firmly attached to a fixed structure at one end and is unsupported at the other end. It can be a beam, plate, truss, or slab, and it sometimes projects from a vertical surface such as a wall. When a load sits at the far, unsupported end, the cantilever carries that load to the support as shear stress and a bending moment. Cantilever construction allows overhanging structures without external support, including cantilever bridges and balconies. [wikipedia.org · nih.gov]
  • Truss. A truss is an assembly of members such as beams, connected by nodes, that creates a rigid structure. Members are two-force members: force is applied to only two points, and the assemblage behaves as a single object. Architectural trusses typically comprise five or more triangular units, because a triangle will not change shape when the lengths of the sides are fixed. The top beams are called top chords and are typically in compression, and the bottom beams are called bottom chords and are typically in tension. [wikipedia.org · loc.gov]
