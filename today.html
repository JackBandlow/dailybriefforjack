How to structure a prompt for a generative model

Prompt engineering is the practice of designing and refining natural language prompts given to a generative AI model to produce more accurate, relevant, or useful output. A prompt can state the task, add relevant context, specify the style of the output, and assign a role for the model to play. The usual techniques include few-shot prompting, which supplies examples, and chain-of-thought prompting, in which the model generates a series of intermediate reasoning steps. The performance of large language models can change with small variations in phrasing and with the order of examples.

Sources: wikipedia.org · arxiv.org

How to clear your head with Getting Things Done

Getting Things Done is a personal productivity system developed by David Allen. The method is to move tasks and projects out of your mind into an external record, then break them into actionable work items. The workflow has five stages: capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage. After you capture incoming material, decide the next action and park reminders in a trusted system that you review at least weekly; Allen also advises starting with a paper-based system.

Today's field: Science & technology

  • Plate tectonics. Plate tectonics is the theory that Earth's outermost layer is broken into large and small plates that move relative to one another. A plate, in geologic terms, is a large, rigid slab of solid rock that rides atop hotter, more mobile material below. Where the plates meet, their relative motion can be apart, together, or sliding past each other, and those boundaries are where earthquakes, volcanic activity, and mountain-building are common. [wikipedia.org · usgs.gov]
  • Semiconductor. A semiconductor is a material with electrical conductivity between that of a conductor and an insulator. Its conductivity can be modified by adding impurities, a process called doping, and junctions between regions with different doping levels are the basis of diodes, transistors, and most modern electronics. Silicon is a critical element for fabricating most electronic circuits, and semiconductors are the base for the integrated circuits, or chips, used in computing and communications. [wikipedia.org · nist.gov]
  • Quantum entanglement. Quantum entanglement is the phenomenon in which the quantum state of each particle in a group cannot be described independently of the state of the others, even when the particles are separated by a large distance. The correlations between those separated quantum systems are nonclassical, unlike the correlations of ordinary classical physics. Entanglement can be measured and used as a resource: a pair of quantum systems in an entangled state can perform computational and cryptographic tasks that are impossible for classical systems. [wikipedia.org · stanford.edu]
