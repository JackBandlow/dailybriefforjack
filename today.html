How to treat unexpected credential requests as phishing

Phishing is a form of social engineering in which attackers deceive people into revealing sensitive information or installing malware. Attacks are often delivered by email and can involve messages that appear to be from a trusted source, such as a bank or government agency, then redirect to a counterfeit login page that collects credentials. The same approach also appears in voice calls, called vishing, and in text messages, called smishing. Measures that reduce the impact include user education, public awareness, and technical security measures.

Sources: wikipedia.org · doi.org

How to space reviews instead of cramming

Spaced repetition is a learning technique, usually performed with flashcards, that shows new and more difficult items more often and older, less difficult items less often, in order to exploit the spacing effect. Spaced training with long intervals between sessions leads to more robust memory formation than massed training with short or no intervals. When you need to retain many items, review on an expanding schedule: if you recall an item correctly, wait longer before the next review; if you do not, go back to a shorter interval.

Today's field: AI & machine learning

  • Diffusion model. A diffusion model has two parts: a forward process that adds noise to data, and a reverse sampling process that learns to undo that noise. The goal is to generate new examples that are distributed similarly to the original dataset. A typical image model is trained to sequentially denoise images corrupted by Gaussian noise, then generates by starting from random noise and denoising step by step. [wikipedia.org · acm.org]
  • Gradient descent. Gradient descent is an iterative method for minimizing a differentiable function by taking repeated steps in the opposite direction of the gradient, which is the direction of steepest descent. In machine learning it is used to minimize a cost or loss function. A step size that is too small slows convergence, and a step size that is too large can overshoot and diverge. [wikipedia.org · doi.org]
  • Overfitting. Overfitting is the production of an analysis that corresponds too closely or exactly to a particular set of data and may therefore fail to predict future observations reliably. An overfitted model contains more parameters than can be justified by the data, and may memorize training data rather than learn to generalize. Techniques that lessen overfitting include cross-validation, regularization, early stopping, and testing the model on data not used for training. [wikipedia.org · utexas.edu]
