Update: Humboldt County orders King Salmon evacuation as seawater floods streets

The county designated the Humboldt Hill Grange in Eureka as an evacuation point, with a separate overnight shelter at the Bear River Recreation Center in Loleta.

Level Up

Verify an unexpected account alert outside the message

A convincing sign-in alert can be real or a lure. If the message is unexpected, open the service from a bookmark, its official app, or an address you already know, rather than using the message’s link or phone number. Check security activity there. Do not reply with a password or a verification code. If something looks wrong, use the service’s documented recovery options and report the suspicious message through your email client.

Sources: Federal Trade Commission · Google Account Help

Start a clock when the refrigerator loses power

If the electricity goes out, mark the time and keep the refrigerator and freezer doors closed. An unopened refrigerator generally keeps food cold for about four hours. If the outage is longer, check official guidance before keeping refrigerated perishables such as meat, eggs or milk; don’t taste food to determine whether it is safe. Keeping an appliance thermometer and ice or frozen packs on hand makes it easier to act on temperature rather than guess.

Sources: Food and Drug Administration · Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Today's field: Law (U.S. legal concepts)

  • Judicial notice of a fact. Federal Rule of Evidence 201 lets a court accept certain case-specific facts without ordinary proof when they are not reasonably disputable. A party can ask to be heard on whether notice is proper; the rule is not a shortcut to resolving a genuinely disputed fact. [Federal Rule of Evidence 201 · Cornell Legal Information Institute]
  • Request for admission. In a federal civil case, Rule 36 lets one party ask another to admit a fact, an application of law to fact, or a document’s authenticity. It narrows disputes rather than taking testimony; a matter admitted is generally established for that case unless the court allows withdrawal or amendment. [Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 36 · Cornell Legal Information Institute]
  • Permission to appeal before final judgment. Federal appeals normally follow a final decision. Under Section 1292(b), a qualifying civil order can be certified for possible early review, and the appeals court still decides whether to permit the appeal. Certification does not itself decide the merits. [28 U.S.C. Section 1292 · Cornell Legal Information Institute]
