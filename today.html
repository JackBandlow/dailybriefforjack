Justice Department asks Supreme Court to block Venezuelan TPS ruling

The Justice Department on Wednesday asked the Supreme Court to pause a ruling that the administration lacked authority to end Temporary Protected Status for hundreds of thousands of Venezuelan migrants — the government's third appeal on the issue. Science & Health

FDA approves first drug for neurological symptoms of Type 3 Gaucher disease

The FDA approved Sanofi's once-daily oral drug Infilbray (venglustat) as the first treatment for neurological symptoms of Type 3 Gaucher disease in adults and children weighing at least 15 kilograms (33 pounds), the company said Wednesday. The rare inherited disorder can cause difficulties with coordination, speech and thinking as harmful substances build up in the brain. Sanofi said a late-stage trial showed significant improvement in some neurological symptoms. The label warns of suicidal ideation and behavior, lens opacification in pediatric patients, and embryo-fetal toxicity.

Late Homo erectus fossils in southern China suggest rainforest survival

Two Nature studies published Wednesday say Homo erectus may have lived in southern China's tropical forests until 184,000 years ago. Researchers identified eight of nine hominin teeth from the Baxian and Naxian caves, about 15 kilometers apart in Chongzuo, Guangxi, as Homo erectus and dated them to 324,000–184,000 years ago, the youngest securely dated remains in China and at least 80,000 years later than other mainland fossils, previously known only from before about 400,000 years. Tooth-enamel isotopes indicated a rainforest habitat.

Level Up

Space reviews out instead of cramming

Spaced repetition is an evidence-based way to learn: practice the same material with longer gaps between sessions, rather than packing the practice into one sitting, known as massed training. A review available from the National Institutes of Health finds that spaced training forms more robust memory than massed training, a result noted as early as Ebbinghaus and later shown for facts, concepts, vocabulary, and skills. A common method is flashcards: show new or harder cards more often, and older, easier cards less often, to use the spacing effect. If you need to remember many items for the long term, schedule shorter reviews over time instead of one long cram session.

Sources: nih.gov · wikipedia.org

Treat unexpected messages as possible phishing

Phishing is a form of social engineering in which attackers deceive people into revealing sensitive information or installing malware, including for credential theft. A 2024 systematic review reports that phishing is an important security threat that can cause financial and reputational losses, and that purely technical defenses are never perfect; human decision-making is the final barrier. Attacks often arrive by email and may be personalized. Before you enter login credentials or act on an unexpected message, look for phishing indicators and do not rely on technical filters alone.

Sources: doi.org · wikipedia.org
