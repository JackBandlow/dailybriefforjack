ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

The International Court of Justice ruled Thursday that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 Rakhine state military operations, the first such finding against a state. In a 9-6 decision, President Yuji Iwasawa said Myanmar violated the Genocide Convention. The court rejected Myanmar's defense that the campaign was a legitimate operation against insurgents and said security forces killed an estimated 10,000 Rohingya; more than 700,000 fled to Bangladesh. The Gambia brought the 2019 case. The court also found Myanmar failed to prevent and punish genocide and ordered restitution.

Update: NASA confirms Voyager 1's signal from a light-day away

Thaw turkey in the fridge, not on the counter

Iowa State University Extension, citing USDA guidance, says never thaw a turkey on the counter, garage, porch, or bathtub: once the outer layer rises above 40°F, it sits in the 40–140°F "danger zone," where bacteria multiply quickly. Allow about 24 hours in a refrigerator at 40°F or below for every 4 to 5 pounds, or thaw in cold water. Use a food thermometer to verify 165°F in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost wing and thigh, per USDA advice also reported by KCHA News.

Sources: iastate.edu · kchanews.com

Today's field: Science & technology

  • Greenhouse effect. The greenhouse effect happens when certain gases in a planet's atmosphere slow the loss of heat to space and raise the surface temperature. Columbia University lecture notes explain that Earth's atmosphere is nearly transparent to incoming sunlight, but water vapor and carbon dioxide absorb infrared radiation emitted by the warmed surface and radiate some of that energy back downward. The notes calculate an "effective temperature" of about -18°C (255 K) if Earth had no atmosphere, compared with an average surface temperature of about 15°C (288 K). [columbia.edu · nasa.gov]
  • mRNA vaccine. An mRNA vaccine delivers antigen-encoding messenger RNA into cells, which use it to make a protein like one from a virus or other pathogen; that protein then triggers an immune response. The RNA is typically carried in lipid nanoparticles that help protect it and get it into cells. An NIH-hosted review notes that, unlike DNA vaccines, mRNA does not integrate into the host genome. [nih.gov · cdc.gov]
  • Antimicrobial resistance. A World Health Organization report, prepared with the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control, says some common hospital bacteria no longer respond to standard antibiotics, leading doctors to turn to broader drugs such as carbapenems, which also face resistance in some countries. [emjreviews.com · who.int]
